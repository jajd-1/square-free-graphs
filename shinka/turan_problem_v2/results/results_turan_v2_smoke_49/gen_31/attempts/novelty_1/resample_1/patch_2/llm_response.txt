<DESCRIPTION>
The current augmentation penalizes every endpoint degree above six, despite dense C4-free graphs on 49 vertices typically requiring many degree-seven vertices to reach the best edge counts. The ordinary endpoint-load term already discourages excessive hubs. Moving the explicit excess penalty threshold to seven allows useful seventh incident edges while retaining a stronger penalty once degree eight is reached. This only changes the ranking of already legal insertions; the C4-free invariant and all explicit validation remain untouched.
</DESCRIPTION>
